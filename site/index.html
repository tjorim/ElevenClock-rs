Everything a taskbar clock should be

Any format you like

Pick a preset or write your own with simple tokens: hours, minutes, seconds, weekdays, months, calendar years and ISO weeks, on one line or two. A live preview checks it before it reaches your taskbar.

Featherlight

About 3 MiB of memory and around 0.01% CPU. No busy loop: the clock only redraws when its text changes.

Never breaks your clock

The Windows clock is never changed or disabled. ElevenClock-rs draws on top of it, so clicking still opens the calendar, and exiting brings the original straight back.

Every display

Show your clock on the primary taskbar or on every monitor, at any scaling. It follows auto-hide, full-screen apps and Explorer restarts.

One small native app

Written in Rust as a single executable: no Python, Qt or runtime to install. Installs for your account without administrator rights, or runs portable.

Private and accessible

No internet access and no telemetry. Works with screen readers, the keyboard and high contrast. Light and dark themes follow Windows.

Settings that explain themselves

Every change is previewed against tricky dates, like the week that spans New Year. A format that is invalid or does not fit the taskbar is explained and never applied. Apply, Cancel and Reset work the way you expect.

Start at sign-in is off until you turn it on, and diagnostics are only ever copied to your clipboard, never sent anywhere.

Install in a minute

  1. Download ElevenClock-rs-…-x64-setup.exe from the latest release.
  2. Run it. It installs for your account only, without administrator rights.
  3. Open ElevenClock-rs from the Start menu and pick your format.

Prefer no installer? The release also has a portable ZIP: extract it and double-click elevenclock-rs.exe.

Releases are not code-signed yet, so Windows may say “Windows protected your PC”. Choose More info, then Run anyway. Each release lists SHA-256 checksums.

Coming from the original ElevenClock? Quit it and switch it off in Settings > Apps > Startup first; the migration guide explains what changed.

Questions

Does it change or hide the Windows clock?

No. It draws its own clock on top of the Windows one and never modifies, disables or hides it. Exit ElevenClock-rs, or pause it from the tray, and the Windows clock is right there.

What do I need?

Stock Windows 11 on an x64 PC. Windows 10, ARM64 devices, Insider builds, taskbar replacements such as ExplorerPatcher or StartAllBack, and taskbars on the side or top of the screen are not supported.

What is an ISO week date?

A compact way to write a day as year, week and weekday: 2642.2 is 2026, week 42, Tuesday. Weeks start on Monday and week 1 contains the year's first Thursday, so the week-year can differ from the calendar year around New Year.

How does it relate to the original ElevenClock?

ElevenClock-rs is an independent Rust rewrite, inspired by ElevenClock by Martí Climent. It focuses on a custom format with a tiny footprint; some features of the original, like internet time sync and translations, are not included. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the original project.

How do I uninstall it?

Choose Exit ElevenClock-rs in Settings or the tray menu, then uninstall from Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps. It also removes its own startup entry.

Something is wrong. Where do I get help?

Open Settings > Support, click Copy diagnostics and paste it into a new issue. The report contains no user name, paths or window titles.
